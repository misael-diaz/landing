    • STEM Tutoring

      Teaching Philosophy

      Students are eager to learn what interest them, and so, my key to teaching them is to persuade them early on about the importance of the field of study. After having students understand the importance of what they are learning, I have seen them engage with the subject more intently out of the own volition. To me there's nothing more rewarding than seeing my students take off on their own and become their own teachers and the teachers of their peers.

      Services

      • One hour session for concept learning and reinforcement with the student.
      • One hour session for problem solving with the student.
      • One to two-hour sessions dedicated to the development of scientific fair or engineering project.
      • STEM lab projects that could encompass a combination of web development, embedded devices programming, game development and any other areas of interest to the student.
