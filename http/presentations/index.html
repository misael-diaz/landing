Motivation

I used to love Google Docs suite to the point that I never thought that I would use something else to prepare my presentations. I had the convenience of the cloud, free storage, versioning, and could even work offline if needed. Searching for files was not great but I had the discipline to use directories to organize my files. I had it all figured it out. I even encouraged my students to use Google Docs as a great alternative to Microsoft products. So why the switch, what was the reason for switching to creating my presentations to the unlikely alternative of doing it all myself with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript? You probably guessed it and so it's not necessary to say more.

I wish Google had the wisdom to allow users to opt out from features in their products that nobody asked for. Or at least keep those unwanted features inconspicuous; instead those features beg to be used every single time you make a change to a document. I simply got tired of all those interruptions, it's like having a person commenting outloud what you should do next when you are already in a deep focus state while making sure the content looks exactly the way you want.

To my dismal the software I used to love became something else and I had no choice but to move on. Google I know exactly how I want my documents to look like and I am comfortable writing my documents character by character just like in the good old days where people used typewriters.

Now I am working in the quietness and comfort provided my favorite text editor, vim of course. I am in control of every character and every pixel that makes up my presentations with just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The best part is that I have not given up all the features that I used to love about Google Docs. I have versioning through git and free cloud storage through GitHub. One may argue that my experience has worsen because now I need to be connected to the internet to create my presentations. I would just respond to that by saying that I have implemented my own development http server from scratch with C/C++, and so I can easily work offline. And I have a better searching experience because now I can leverage the Unix commands that every programmer should know to search for my files and even match them by their contents. I already had all the pieces and the experience to do this myself, Google just gave me the knudge that I needed to know that it was time to leave.
